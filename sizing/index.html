Method and assumptions

Weight

Gross weight is iterated from W₀ = (payload + crew + fuel) / (1 − We/W₀). Payload 100 kg per passenger, 2 crew at 85 kg. Empty-weight fraction 0.58 for main + tail rotor and 0.61 for coaxial, which carries a second hub, longer mast and coaxial gearbox.

Rotor

Disk loading DL = 6.0·W0.23 kg/m², fitted to the AW139 and S-92. Tip speed 215 m/s, blade loading CT/σ = 0.08. Five blades for the single rotor and 4 + 4 for the coaxial. Tail rotor diameter is 0.195 D. Coaxial rotor spacing is 0.09 D.

Power

Momentum theory for induced power (κ = 1.15), profile power with Cd0 = 0.010 and the (1 + 4.65μ²) term, and parasite power from flat-plate area f = 0.0035·W2/3 m². Coaxial: 1.16 interference factor in hover, and 30 % more f for the twin hubs and mast. The tail rotor takes about 11 % of main-rotor power in hover, less in cruise.

Engines, fuel, speed

  • Installed power is 1.6 × hover power (twin-engine, OEI margin).
  • SFC is 0.30 kg/kWh, with a 30-minute reserve.
  • Maximum speed is whichever is lower: the speed where power required meets maximum continuous power, or the rotor limit.
  • Lift-offset rotors and pusher-propeller compounds are not modelled.

CAD files

STEP and STL models of the baseline (16 passengers, 800 km, 145 kt) are in cad/out/ in the repository. To regenerate them for other requirements, run node sizing/run_sizing.js 16 800 145 and then python3 cad/build_cad.py.
